Eligibility Criteria

Undergraduate Admission (UG)

  • Students must complete 10+2 education.
  • Students must meet the required subject criteria.
  • Students must satisfy the minimum qualifying marks.

Postgraduate Admission (PG)

  • Students must have a relevant bachelor's degree.
  • Students must meet the university admission requirements.

Admission Procedure

  1. Visit the university admission portal.
  2. Select your preferred programme.
  3. Fill out the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Wait for admission confirmation.
